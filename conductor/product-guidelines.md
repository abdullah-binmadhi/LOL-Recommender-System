# Product Guidelines

## Tone and Voice
- **Balanced Communication:** Blend professional analytical data with a friendly, encouraging voice.
- **Data-Driven but Approachable:** Present technical insights (e.g., "94% Confidence") alongside supportive language (e.g., "A great fit for your style!").
- **Competitive Motivation:** Maintain an energetic tone that inspires players to improve and master their recommended champions.

## Visual Identity & Design
- **Clean Compact Design:** Prioritize a high-density, information-rich layout that minimizes scrolling and excessive padding.
- **Simplified Color Palette:** Use a restrained color palette (e.g., simple greys, blues) instead of complex gradients or thematic "hextech" visuals to improve readability.
- **Professional Aesthetic:** The interface should feel like a serious analytical tool, avoiding "gamified" or flashy design elements.

## Data Visualization
- **Progressive Disclosure:** Use simplified visual cues like color-coded progress bars and badges for immediate understanding.
- **Interactive Depth:** Provide hover states and expandable sections to reveal detailed technical scores (Decision Tree, KNN, Random Forest) for power users.
- **Consistency:** Ensure colors consistently represent the same algorithms or roles throughout the application.

## Accessibility & Usability
- **Responsive Design:** Maintain a mobile-first approach where the questionnaire and results are fully functional on small screens.
- **Standard Compliance:** Ensure high color contrast and readable font sizes for all users.
- **Performance:** Optimize for lightning-fast interactions (sub-20ms processing) to provide instantaneous feedback.
